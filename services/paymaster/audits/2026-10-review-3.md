# Internal review #3 (2026-10): PepuSponsoredPaymasterV7 and the signer service

Internal, AI-assisted review at `4882bd3` by three parallel auditors (pashov method), with the fixes made afterwards. It follows `2026-10-internal-review.md` and `2026-10-delta-review.md`. This is not an external audit.

Three auditors ran in parallel:

- **A:** economics, accounting and math.
- **B:** access control and governance.
- **C:** ERC-4337 conformance and the signer service.

All three ran against the real vendored EntryPoint v0.7, which is byte-identical to upstream (`eth-infinitism` v0.7.0).

**Totals after dedupe:** 0 Critical, 0 High, 3 Medium, 6 Low, and several Info items and leads.

**What no auditor could break:**

- Ledger solvency. Stateful fuzzing on the real EntryPoint found zero violations.
- Fund theft.
- Signature forgery or replay. 80 differential vectors confirm the signed-hash parity between the contract and the service.
- Bypassing the on-chain limits.
- Any regression of earlier fixes.

## Fix status

All Medium and Low findings are fixed, together with the Info items B5, C-L1 and A7 and the openapi gaps. The rest are documented in `SPONSORSHIP-SPEC.md` section 16.3. The auditors' PoCs were brought into the test suites and adapted so the attack now fails.

Contract runtime size after the fixes: **23,722 bytes** (854 under EIP-170; it was 24,405). Getting there needed the sponsored contract to be compiled with **8,000 optimizer runs** (25,115 bytes at 1,000,000). Measured on the real-EntryPoint harness with cold state: validation 72,237 -> 72,393 gas (+0.2%), worst-case postOp 13,843 -> 13,918 (+0.5%). The size levers in order, with the byte count after each, are in `SPONSORSHIP-SPEC.md` 16.2. ## Original report (as consolidated from the three auditors)

### Medium

- **M-1 (B1). A funded, unclaimed project has no pause defence.**
  - Attack: a compromised manager proposes a rogue signer and bundler, waits 48 h, then claims the project and drains it. The PoC drained 85 of 100 PEPU.
  - Defence at the time: the owner cancels inside the window. After a renounce there is none.
  - Suggested fix: make the first claim of a project with `balance > 0` pending (`adminChangeDelay`), signer-bound and cancellable by the contract owner or manager. A claim of an empty project stays immediate. Optionally cap `setMaxFeePerGas` at `defaultMaxFeePerGas`.
- **M-2 (B2). A hostile current owner can veto recovery forever with `cancelReclaim`.**
  - Scenario: a leaked signer front-runs the first claim of a funded project, so attacker E becomes owner. Ops then removes the signer, and the honest team runs `reclaimProject` five times. E cancels each attempt at about 47 hours. Accepting a self-nominated transfer also clears `pendingReclaim`. No owner or manager tie-breaker exists. The same applies to a compromised owner key (T10) and to two Privy-secret holders racing a first claim.
  - Fix options: (a) one veto per (project, newOwner); (b) a guardian that can force an attested reclaim; (c) allow cancelling only in the first half of the delay. Option (a) was implemented.
- **M-3 (C1). Service DoS: unthrottled denials write SQLite audit rows.**
  - `deny()` always wrote an `appendAudit` row as a synchronous SQLite insert. `/v2/sponsor` had no limiter, and the ERC-7677 limiter was keyed per token, so junk tokens dodged it. `audit_log_v2` was never pruned.
  - Evidence: PoC 2 sent 1,500 junk keys and got 1,500 rows written, at about 436 requests/s from one client; 100 random tokens were never limited. The service fails closed on a DB error, so a full disk stops all sponsorship.

### Low

- **L-1 (A2 + C4, two auditors). Projects get no per-wallet limit after a claim.** `senderDailyLimit` defaults to 0, which means "off", contradicting spec 7a ("required > 0"). One wallet can burn the whole `dailyLimit`.
- **L-2 (A3 + B3, two auditors). Manager `addStake` cancels the owner's unlock and can set a 136-year delay.** `addStake` was `onlyOwnerOrManager` while `unlockStake` and `withdrawStake` are `onlyOwner`; `addStake(uint32.max)` with value 0 re-locks stake the owner already unlocked, and the delay can never be decreased.
- **L-3 (B4). `removeBundlers([address(0)])` is irreversible and breaks estimation.** `proposeAdminChange` rejected the zero account, so once `address(0)` was removed it could not be re-added, which breaks `tx.origin == 0` estimation permanently.
- **L-4 (C2). Replacing an approval for the same (sender, nonce) undercounts signed exposure off-chain.** Approval A is voided when B is requested for the same (sender, nonce), but A's signature stays valid for 60 s. By alternating expensive and cheap requests, an attacker could hold many valid expensive signatures while the off-chain caps only counted the cheap ones. The on-chain limits still held.
- **L-5 (C3). `allowAccountDeployment=false` is not enforced on `/v2/sponsor`.** Only the ERC-7677 route checked it.
- **L-6 (A1). The real worst-case postOp is 16.2k to 18.1k gas, against an 18,000 floor.** It was still solvent, but only through the penalty margin. Suggested fix: snapshot `postOpOverheadGas` and a `senderTracked` flag into the context (saves 11 bytes and drops the worst case to about 13.8k); also correct the spec numbers and move `setPostOpOverheadGas` into `setUp` in the real-EntryPoint tests.

### Info and leads

- **B5.** Nominations, reclaims and admin proposals never expire, and a nominee cannot be unset.
- **C-L1.** `POST /v2/projects` falls back to `body.appId` without checking `keccak(appId) == projectId`.
- **C-L2.** Owner routes rescan `eth_getLogs` from `deployBlock`. Add a cursor.
- **A4.** A constant overcharge of about 11.2k gas per op flows to the free pool. A tighter penalty bound is possible.
- **A5.** There is a midnight liveness gap from stale-day `validAfter`.
- **A6.** The immutable 40k gwei fee cap becomes a liveness hazard if the base fee spikes. The free pool cannot be fixed after a renounce.
- **A7.** In `setFreeTier`, `senderDailyLimit < maxOpCost` makes the free tier unusable. Validate it.
- **A8.** Inventory of stranded funds under deposit-only.
- **Free-pool sybil cost.** About 50 PEPU per verified Privy account per day, with no aggregate bound by design. Fund the pool in tranches.
- **B leads:**
  - `setFreeTargets` is instant, with no timelock.
  - The owner can self-bundle the free pool after 48 h.
  - A reclaim inherits the previous owner's config.
  - Signers must be EOAs.
  - A fee cap cannot be reset to the default.
- **C info:**
  - A conformant ERC-7562 bundler would reject this paymaster (ORIGIN opcode, global slot 15 storage, stake required). It needs a self-run Alto with safe-mode off. Document this as a hard requirement.
  - `TRUST_PROXY` and `TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS` must match the deployment.
  - Clock-skew edge case at the maximum claim TTL.
  - openapi was missing 400/413 responses.
  - A nonce above 2^256 returns 500.
  - The Privy cross-app secret check is still untested live.

- **M-B1.** `claimProject`, `setMaxFeePerGas`, `proposeAdminChange`, `executeAdminChange`. An unclaimed project has no owner, so nobody can pause it or cap its fees. A compromised manager proposes a rogue signer and bundler, waits 48 h, executes both, has the rogue signer attest a claim for the attacker (immediate), then the attacker sets its own targets, limits and `setMaxFeePerGas(max)` and self-bundles. 85 of 100 PEPU reached the attacker's beneficiary address. - **M-B2.** `cancelReclaim` veto, see M-2.
- **L-B3.** Manager `addStake(uint32.max)` with value 0 re-locks the owner's unlocked stake and the delay can never be decreased: the 100 PEPU stake is stuck.
- **L-B4.** `removeBundlers([address(0)])`, see L-3.
- **L/I-B5.** Nothing expires; a stale nominee can accept years later, take the project and wipe a recovery re-claim in the process.
- Leads (not scored): `setFreeTargets` instant; the owner can drain the free pool and the T15 residue via self-bundling after 48 h (donors to the free pool cannot exit); `executeReclaim` inherits the old owner's allowlist, codehash pin and limits; a rogue signer can front-run `removeSigner` by claiming funded projects first; after a renounce a compromised manager cannot be recovered from and `pauseFreePool` cannot be undone; signers must be EOAs; a fee cap cannot be reset to 0.
- Ruled out: spending an unclaimed project without a signer; using the free pool as a project; `signers[0]`; timelock bypass; front-running propose or execute; attestation replay or redirect; an off-by-one in `signerRemovedAt`; desync between a pending transfer and a pending reclaim; pause missing a spend path; direct theft by the owner; renounce footguns; spending across projects; `tx.origin == 0` on Nitro; a phished allowlisted bundler; griefing a claim; the manager raising the overhead or re-enabling the free pool. No regressions: the delta-review fixes M1, L2 and L3 still hold.

**Auditor C (ERC-4337 conformance and the signer service):** 0 Critical, 0 High, 1 Medium, 3 Low, 2 leads, 7 Info; baselines forge 171 and vitest 246. 6 Foundry tests, 80 differential hash vectors and 5 vitest PoCs. No path was found that moves ledger funds, forges a sponsor signature, replays an approval, or bypasses the on-chain limits.

- **I1. A conformant ERC-7562 bundler would reject this paymaster.** The supported setup is a self-run bundler with safe-mode off and an allowlisted executor. The three reasons: (a) `ORIGIN` is a banned opcode in validation; (b) it accesses unassociated storage (`sponsors`, the global `totalLiabilities` in slot 15 written by every op so ops conflict with each other, `targetAllowed` / `signers` / `isBundlerAllowed`), which needs a staked paymaster; (c) calling `unlockStake` removes the stake that (b) depends on.
- **I2. Alto 0.0.21 with safe-mode off** uses `UnsafeValidator` and `NullReputationManager`: no trace validation, no opcode or storage rules, no reputation. The expiry check is `validUntil < now+5`, so the 60 s window is usable for about 55 s. This is an operator risk (bundler gas), not a risk to user funds. It matches T20.
- **I3. The signed-hash parity is byte-exact.** All 80 differential vectors match, and every hashed field is bound to the signature.
- **I4. Signature handling is strict:** exactly 65 bytes, low-s, `v` in {27, 28}. A zero signer is harmless.
- **I5. Only `paymasterAndData` of length 162 parses.** Every other length gets a typed revert.
- **I6. PostOpMode:** `opReverted` refunds the same sponsor. `postOpReverted` only follows a `uint128` overflow or out-of-gas, and the residue goes to surplus. `INNER_REVERT_LOW_PREFUND` is solvent.
- **I7. Operational notes:** `TRUST_PROXY` / `TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS` must match the real proxy setup, or a single caller can exhaust the shared IP buckets; at the maximum claim TTL of 3,600 s clock skew can trip `ClaimDeadlineTooFar` (the default of 600 s is fine); the verify limiter runs before auth; the verify failure budget is peek-then-charge, which races under concurrency (the per-IP limit still bounds it); a nonce above 2^256-1 returns a 500; the Privy cross-app secret check is still untested live.
- Ruled out: cross-chain, cross-paymaster or cross-sponsor replay; confusing claim signatures with sponsorship signatures (EIP-712 vs EIP-191); signature malleability; a zero signer; a mid-bundle surplus credit; forcing `postOp` to revert, given the 40k floor against about 14.6k worst case; a difference between how the paymaster and Kernel decode batch calls (the paymaster's decoder is stricter, so it fails closed); a third party including a sponsored op, since the `tx.origin` gate is unconditional; abuse of `tx.origin == 0`; bypassing the L4 revocation; SIWE replay or ERC-6492; admin token timing attacks; concurrent double-spend, since `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` is synchronous; duplicate credential issuance; a Privy secret leak; using Origin as authentication; bypassing caps through the idempotent skip; a modified EntryPoint.

**Auditor A (economics, accounting and math):** stateful fuzzing on the real EntryPoint v0.7 (honest ops, slot-restoring ops, restore plus a mid-op `senderDailyLimit` flip, two-op bundles across sponsors, priority fee below max fee, basefee changes, midnight warps, surplus credits, EntryPoint donations, stake add/unlock/withdraw, daily-limit changes, with `postOpOverheadGas` at its floor) found zero solvency violations. Findings A1 (L-6), A2 (L-1), A3 (L-2), A4 to A8 as listed above. The postOp gas was measured with an instrumented copy of the EntryPoint that emits `preGas - gasleft()` around the `postOp` call and changes no accounting. Measured by the auditor on the code before the fixes: 16.2k for a slot-restoring op and 18.1k for restore plus a mid-op `senderDailyLimit` flip, against the 18,000 floor.
