Pillar 3

Anatomy of a sponsored transaction

1

Client builds a UserOperation

The Kernel account (0 PEPU) is the sender. useSponsoredSend or useDirectSponsoredSend.

2

Paymaster stub data, then final data

ERC-7677 against paymaster.pepeunchained.com: pm_getPaymasterStubData, gas estimate, then pm_getPaymasterData, the signed approval.

3

Owner signs, bundler receives

eth_sendUserOperation to bundler.pepeunchained.com (ERC-4337, EntryPoint v0.7).

4

EntryPoint validates, executes, charges

Bundler submits handleOps. The v2 paymaster contract 0xdb28…C30D pays from its EntryPoint deposit. The account never needs PEPU.

5

Transaction lands on PEPU mainnet

Look it up on pepuscan.com by the smart account address, the sender.

No paymaster approval, no transaction: the policy service signs only if every rule passes.onboarding/sponsored-transactions
